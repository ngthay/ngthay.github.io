About me

I'm Eang Eng Thay, a civil engineer based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, currently working as a Structural Design Engineer at Rabiica Construction and Design.

I have worked as a site engineer, draftsman, site inspector and design engineer on buildings ranging from schools to a 54-storey tower, which gives me a practical view of how a design is drawn, checked and built.

I graduated in Civil Engineering from the National Technical Training Institute (NTTI) in 2022 and keep learning new tools and methods to deliver safe, efficient structures.
